# SmithersBot Creative Direction

## Vibe

Bright, happy, new-retro software demo energy.

Old TV show opening meets cutting-edge agent tooling.

The contrast is the joke, but the product should still feel real.

## Emotional reference

Use the jingle and bot avatar as the emotional reference.

Playful, confident, upbeat, a little absurd, but cleanly executed.

## Song hook

SmithersBot, let it roll  
Keep your control  
SmithersBot, hands stay free  
Agents run, you run the key

Leave agents running  
Without giving up control

## Tone

Light and memorable, not goofy.

Confident, not try-hard.

Technical, but not cold.

A little theatrical, but never cluttered.

## Visual direction

Use a warm, bright base with crisp product proof.

Telegram should feel like the remote control.

The serious parts should be the plan, checkpoints, verification, and execution trail.

## Good signals

- real screenshots
- clean section headers
- warm retro color accents
- polished type hierarchy
- compact diagrams
- tasteful motion
- enough humor to be memorable
- enough restraint to be trusted

## Bad signals

- generic AI SaaS gradients
- fake dashboards
- fake terminal logs
- invented product screenshots
- robot mascots
- too much navigation
- too many decorative elements
- overexplaining the joke
- overemphasizing OpenClaw/fork history
- making the project look unserious

## Brand balance

The brand can be playful.

The execution cannot be sloppy.
